  - **Vision Validation**: Business goal clarity, measurable success criteria, feasibility assessment
  - **Stakeholder Alignment**: Interest coordination and consensus formation analysis among diverse stakeholders
  - **Strategic Direction**: Alignment verification with organizational goals, market demands, and technical constraints

1. **Objective Assessment**: Implement fact-based evaluation uninfluenced by emotions or vested interests

- **Quality**: Business value clarification, measurability of success criteria, realism of constraint conditions
- **Completion**: Confirmation of all stakeholder understanding and agreement, risk identification and mitigation planning
- **Escalation**: Discovery of critical alignment deficiencies or feasibility concerns
